# Instagram Stories – Zoomout Crew Website Launch (English)

Ready-to-post story ideas and copy. Format: 9:16 (story). Use Canva, Figma, or site screenshots + text.

---

## Story 1 – Hype building

**Visual:** Dark background with cyan/blue/purple gradient (like the site), logo or big text. Teaser vibe — no reveal yet.

**Copy:**

```
Something’s coming ✨

Swipe up or tap for the next story 👆
```

**Alternatives (shorter):**

```
Big news soon 🚀
```

```
We’ve been working on something.
Next story 👆
```

**Tips:** Use a countdown sticker (“New”), “Ask me anything”, or a short clip (drone B‑roll, behind‑the‑scenes) to build curiosity. Keep it vague so the next story hits harder.

## Visual tips

1. **Colours:** cyan (#22d3ee), blue (#3b82f6), purple (#a855f7) — match the site.
2. **Fonts:** clean and modern (e.g. Playfair for headlines, Roboto for body).
3. **Assets:** `/public/assets/logo.png`, `/public/assets/ZOOMOUT LOGO.png`, screenshots from homepage, portfolio, or shop.
4. **Stickers:** “Link” (to bio), “Ask me anything”, countdown “New”.
